🛡️ 1. Introduction & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to GPS Map Camera: Geotag Photo, developed and operated by Eduplus Creation ("we", "our", or "us"). We build professional field inspection, engineering survey, and telemetry camera tools.

🔒 100% On-Device Processing Guarantee: All photographs captured, GPS watermarks stamped, videos recorded, and audio memos created are stored strictly inside your mobile device’s local storage. We do NOT upload, collect, synchronize, sell, or rent your private photos or telemetry to any external cloud servers.

📱 2. Permissions & Data Utilization

To provide real-time geotagging and survey camera capabilities, the application requests the following Android runtime permissions:

Permission Technical Android Name Exact Usage & Safeguards
Camera android.permission.CAMERA Required to operate the live camera viewfinder, capture field photos, record video footage, and apply live GPS HUD overlays. Frames are processed in memory and never transmitted externally.
Precise Location ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Used exclusively in the foreground while the camera is active to calculate latitude, longitude, altitude, and accuracy for stamping onto user photos. Location data is never logged or tracked in the background.
Microphone / Audio android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Optional. Used only when recording survey videos with sound or when you choose to record a voice note attachment for an inspection photo.
Storage & Media READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO		 Required to save your finished stamped photos to the Android device photo gallery and export multi-page PDF survey inspection reports.

🌐 3. Third-Party Services & Network APIs

The app integrates only minimal, standard, privacy-compliant third-party SDKs:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim / Open-Meteo: Reverse-geocodes your active GPS coordinates to display friendly locality, city, and weather data on the stamp card. Queries are sent over encrypted HTTPS and do not identify personal user credentials.
  • Google AdMob (Google Play Services): May serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements to support free development. AdMob may collect anonymous advertising IDs, approximate location, and device telemetry according to Google's Advertising Privacy Policies: Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google ML Kit (Vision OCR & QR): Runs completely offline on your device to detect QR codes or text barcodes for instant coordinate verification. Zero image data is sent to cloud servers.

🗑️ 4. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

Because GPS Map Camera does not require user registration, account sign-in, or remote database synchronization:

  • You retain 100% complete ownership of all media files and surveyor notes created within the app.
  • Deleting a photograph from your device's gallery or within the in-app gallery permanently wipes that photograph and its embedded GPS watermark.
  • Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally cached app configurations and IndexedDB records.
